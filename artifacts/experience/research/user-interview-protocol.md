# User Interview Protocol — Writing Feedback and Review Loop

## Purpose

Validate the learner problem before treating the Error Graph loop as product truth.

## Recruitment

- 12–15 IELTS learners; target band 5.0–7.0; exam within 1–6 months.
- Include at least 4 people who have paid for preparation/feedback and 4 who use free resources only.
- Do not recruit only friends or only users enthusiastic about AI.
- Obtain consent; never commit essay/audio to this repository.

## Interview structure (30–40 minutes)

1. Current goal, deadline and learning routine.
2. Last Writing Task 2 they completed: what happened next?
3. How they currently receive and act on feedback.
4. Examples of repeated mistakes and whether they can name them.
5. Trust boundary: what would make automated feedback useful or unsafe?
6. Concept test: evidence → fix → review → retest.
7. Pricing/WTP questions only after value/problem discussion.

## Non-leading prompts

- "Walk me through the last time you received feedback on an essay."
- "What did you do after you read that feedback?"
- "Which part was useful, confusing or ignored?"
- "How do you know a mistake has actually improved?"
- "What would make you stop using a tool that scores your writing?"

## Evidence to capture

| Signal | Capture | Decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Repeated-error pain | verbatim anonymized note + frequency | Error Graph priority |
| Feedback actionability | concrete follow-up behavior | UX / fix-drill design |
| Trust objection | trigger + required disclosure | confidence/privacy policy |
| Existing spend | category/range, not personal financial details | pricing hypothesis |
| Wedge reaction | what they would replace/keep | positioning |

## Exit criteria

Do not approve a value proposition until at least 10 interviews show recurring evidence for one problem and at least 5 participants describe a concrete current workaround/cost.

## Status

Protocol ready. Findings are intentionally missing until interviews occur; no synthetic interview evidence is permitted.
