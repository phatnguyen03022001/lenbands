Journey • First Day

On first opening the app, the system should make the learner feel “it understands me.”

This journey locks the onboarding, placement, gap analysis, and first entry to home flows. The goal is to reduce uncertainty and create an immediate sense of a path.

Main flow

1. Declare the goalTarget band, test date, and daily study duration.
2. Complete placementUse a quick test or self-report to establish an initial baseline.
3. Analyze the gapsShow which skills are missing and where to start.
4. Create the first planSend the learner to home with a clear next step.
