Journey • Deep Practice

Deep practice should turn mistakes into knowledge that can be recalled.

This journey moves from a difficult question to an explanation, then saves it to the mistake notebook and brings it back through the review queue.

Main flow

1. Do a difficult task / mock testKeep realistic pressure without losing data.
2. Receive feedbackSee the correct answer, distractor trap, and reason for the error immediately.
3. Ask more in contextThe Tutor answers only about the point being studied.
4. Record it in the mistake notebookCreate a loop that brings this item back at the right time.
