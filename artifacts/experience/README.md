# Experience Artifacts

The experience lens owns learner outcomes and behavior: where learners enter, what they see, what they do, how failures appear, and how they complete an outcome.

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `research/` | interview protocols/findings and learner needs |
| `design/` | interaction model, P0 experience contract, IA, navigation, wireframe |
| `specs/` | vertical slice, screen/acceptance behavior spec |

Experience specs do not own OpenAPI, workers, or runtime config. They describe behavior and acceptance; Engineering implements them through contracts, and Operations demonstrates quality/reliability.
