# Licensing Matrix — Baseline Source Policy

- **Status:** research baseline; not approval for individual assets
- **Owner:** Founder
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-06
- **Decision policy:** unknown or ambiguous rights are always handled as `link_only`.

This matrix records the source-level baseline. Each asset still requires rights evidence, intended-use review, and its own manifest before ingestion or publishing.

| Source/group | Default permitted action | Prohibited without written permission/license | Required evidence/notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| IELTS.org / IELTS Partner material | Link to the official source | Copy, republish, modify, commercial reuse, use of IELTS mark/logo, AI ingestion | IELTS copyright/trademark statement; contact the appropriate partner for permission |
| Cambridge content/exam material | Link only | Copy, distribute, adapt, commercial reuse, commercial TDM/AI use | Cambridge terms/copyright policy or written license |
| TED / TED-Ed | Link; standard embed only when terms allow | Commercial reuse, cutting/editing, lesson/transcript derivative, paid redistribution | TED content policy or media license |
| VOA Learning English original material | Conditional; review each item and attribution before use | Republish third-party agency material (AP/Reuters/AFP), use without required credit | VOA page + source check at asset level |
| Content with a clear commercial/open license | Conditional under the exact license | Any use outside license scope, missing attribution/notice, derivative use, or prohibited AI use | Immutable license snapshot + asset manifest |
| First-party / commissioned content | Permitted after contributor/IP agreement and quality review | Use outside contributor agreement or without provenance | Agreement/evidence + asset manifest |
| Generated content | Conditional after provenance, rights, and quality review | Publish content with unresolved source/IP/privacy risk | Workflow record, model/provider terms, review evidence |

## Operating rules

The matrix is not legal advice and does not grant usage rights. Before commercial launch or material use in a new territory, obtain qualified legal review of the intended use and current provider terms.
