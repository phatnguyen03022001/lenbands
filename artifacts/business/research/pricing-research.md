# Pricing Research — MVP Hypotheses

## Purpose

Define pricing research questions, not a final price. Public competitor offers are directional context; local willingness to pay and LenBands unit economics require validation.

## Method and decision status

- **Method:** desk research of public offer pages on 2026-08-06 plus internal cost hypotheses; no price experiment, purchase flow test or learner willingness-to-pay interview has run.
- **Evidence strength:** identifies pricing mechanisms in the market, not an acceptable LenBands price or conversion forecast.
- **Decision status:** no plan, credit package or paywall copy is approved. The only current policy decision is that draft safety and a meaningful free outcome precede upgrade.

## Public anchors (not price recommendations)

| Offer type | Public observation | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Official prep bundle | British Council markets IELTS Ready Premium as included with booking in some contexts | Bundled official prep limits willingness to pay for generic practice alone |
| Course + feedback | E2 lists tiered packages including practice, AI marking, assessments and live support | Feedback quantity/support are common monetization levers |
| Expert course | IELTS Advantage markets a high-ticket course with diagnostic and personal feedback | High price needs trust, structured support and outcome claims buyers can evaluate |

## Pricing hypotheses to test

1. Free tier: placement preview + one meaningful Writing feedback experience, without deceptive lock-in.
2. Paid value: recurring evaluation, Error Graph, review/retest loop and readiness—not raw content access alone.
3. Pricing unit: compare subscription, evaluation credits and exam-window package; do not choose before interviews/pilot usage.
4. Upgrade moment: after learner has seen evidence and one actionable fix, never when a draft is at risk of loss.

## Guardrails

- Never sell an estimated band as official result.
- Quota breach preserves draft and provides a non-destructive alternative.
- No dark pattern countdown, forced annual plan or guilt-based upgrade.
- No price is approved until `cost-budget.md` has numeric ceilings from provider benchmark.
