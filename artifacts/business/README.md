# Business Artifacts

The business lens answers where the product should invest, what value it can charge for, and whether its usage and monetization rights are lawful.

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `research/` | market, competitor, pricing research |
| `decisions/` | build/buy, monetization, or first-class business decisions |
| `legal/` | resource rights, licensing matrix, and commercial/brand/privacy policy from a usage-rights perspective |

Business does not define UX behavior, APIs, or runtime mechanisms. When a decision affects learner experience, engineering, or operations, use capability IDs and `consumed_by`; do not copy the contract here.
