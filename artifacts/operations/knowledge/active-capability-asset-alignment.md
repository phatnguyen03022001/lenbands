## Rules

- Framework controlled vocabulary is authoritative for IELTS semantics.
- A required asset must have schema, provenance, ownership, and lifecycle before release.
- A sample asset is not production coverage.
- No generated or spawned prompt is a learner-content source of truth.
- Missing asset/evidence remains `pending` or `not_ready`; it is never filled with synthetic approval.
