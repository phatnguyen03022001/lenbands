# Second Conditional

## Rule
Second conditional dùng để nói về tình huống **giả định** ở hiện tại hoặc tương lai — việc đó không thật hoặc rất khó xảy ra.

## Form
```
If + past simple, ... would + bare infinitive
```
- If clause: past simple (tất cả động từ, "be" dùng "were" cho mọi ngôi ở formal)
- Main clause: would + bare infinitive (could/might cũng dùng cho khả năng thấp hơn)

**Ví dụ form:**
- If I had more time, I would learn another language.
- If she were here, she would help us.

## Usage
- **Hypothetical present/future**: điều kiện không thật ở hiện tại (If I were rich, I would travel).
- **Advice**: "If I were you, I would..." (thể hiện lời khuyên).
- **Imagination**: tưởng tượng kịch bản không xảy ra.

## Band signal (6.0 → 7.0)
- Band 6.0: có dùng first conditional (If + present, will), ít second conditional.
- Band 7.0: dùng second conditional linh hoạt để:
  - Thể hiện hypothetical reasoning (Task 2 essay về solution).
  - Discuss alternative scenarios (Part 3 Speaking).
  - Đa dạng cấu trúc complex → GRA criterion band 7 ("various complex structures").

Second conditional là 1 trong 5 cấu trúc "must-have" cho band 7.0 GRA (cùng relative clause, passive, perfect tense, subordinate).

## Examples (đúng)
1. If governments invested more in public transport, cities would be less polluted.
2. If I were the minister of education, I would make extracurricular activities compulsory.
3. Without proper regulations, many species would face extinction. (inverted conditional with "without")

## Common errors (liên kết error-taxonomy)

**Error 1: `will` trong if-clause (thay vì past simple)**
- ❌ If I will have money, I would travel.
- ✅ If I had money, I would travel.
- error_id: `W_gra_complex_with_error` (conditional form wrong)
- Fix: if-clause dùng past simple, không "will".

**Error 2: "would" trong if-clause**
- ❌ If I would know the answer, I would tell you.
- ✅ If I knew the answer, I would tell you.
- error_id: `W_gra_complex_with_error`

**Error 3: "was" thay "were" (formal IELTS)**
- ❌ If I was you, I would study harder.  (OK spoken, nhưng IELTS formal nên "were")
- ✅ If I were you, I would study harder.
- error_id: `W_gra_complex_with_error` (register — formal prefer "were")

## Practice gợi ý

1. **Sentence transformation** (5 câu): chuyển từ first conditional sang second conditional.
   - "If I have time, I will help you." → "If I had time, I would help you."

2. **Gap fill** (5 câu): điền đúng form verb.
   - "If I ______ (be) the mayor, I ______ (ban) cars in the city center."

3. **Writing task ứng dụng** (Task 2): viết 1 paragraph về "What would you change about your city?" — yêu cầu dùng ≥2 second conditional.
