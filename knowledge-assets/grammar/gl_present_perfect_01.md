# Present Perfect and Past Simple

## Rule

Use the present perfect for an experience or change connected to the present, and the past simple for a completed event at a finished time.

## Examples

- Public transport has become more reliable in recent years.
- The city introduced the new system in 2024.

## Common error

Do not combine a finished-time expression with the present perfect: use `The council approved the plan last year`, not `has approved ... last year`.

## Practice

Write three sentences about changes in technology and identify whether each sentence connects to the present or a finished past time.
