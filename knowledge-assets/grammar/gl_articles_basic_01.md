# Basic Articles: a, an and the

## Rule

Use `a/an` for one non-specific singular countable noun, `the` for a specific or previously identified noun, and zero article for general plural or uncountable nouns.

## Examples

- A school needs a clear policy before it introduces new software.
- The policy was explained to parents at the meeting.
- Education can shape long-term opportunities.

## Common error

Avoid adding `the` before a general uncountable noun: `Education is important`, not `The education is important` when speaking generally.

## Practice

Complete a short paragraph about education with `a`, `an`, `the` or zero article, then rewrite two sentences with a more specific reference.
